# Benchplan

**Free 3D design for woodworkers, in your browser.** Draw a piece, pull it into solid parts, cut
the joinery, and get a cut list, a shopping list and shop drawings to build from.

**Open the app: [benchplan.dev](https://benchplan.dev)** (nothing to install, no account needed).

This page is where you can report problems, suggest features and follow what's new. The app's
source code is not public.

## What it does

- **Draw and push/pull** faces into boards, panels and other parts, with exact sizes typed in
  metric or imperial (fractions to 1/64").
- **Think in parts:** each part has a name, a material and a grain direction. Copies stay linked,
  so changing one leg changes all four.
- **Joinery:** dados, grooves, rabbets, mortises, tenons and dowel holes, with typed sizes.
- **Build plan:** checks for common mistakes, a cut list, a shopping list (board feet and
  sheets), and shop drawings of every part, ready to print or save as PDF.
- **Exports** for 3D printers (STL, 3MF) and CNC routers and laser cutters (SVG, DXF).

Your designs are saved as files on your own computer and autosaved in your browser. They are not
uploaded anywhere.
